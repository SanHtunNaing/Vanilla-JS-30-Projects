About Animals for Kids 🐾

Scroll down to explore 10 amazing animals and learn fun facts!

01. King of the Jungle

Lion 🦁

Lions are big cats known as the "King of the Jungle." They live in groups called prides and spend up to 20 hours a day resting!

02. Giant Friend

Elephant 🐘

Elephants are the largest land animals on Earth. They use their long trunks to breathe, drink water, and pick up food!

03. Tall & Gentle

Giraffe 🦒

Giraffes are the tallest mammals in the world. Their long necks help them reach juicy leaves high up in acacia trees!

04. Bamboo Lover

Panda 🐼

Giant Pandas spend almost their entire day eating bamboo. They are excellent tree climbers and strong swimmers!

05. Striped Hunter

Tiger 🐯

Tigers are the largest of all wild cats. Unlike most domestic cats, tigers actually love swimming and playing in water!

06. Super Hopper

Kangaroo 🦘

Kangaroos use their powerful back legs to hop around Australia. Mother kangaroos carry their tiny babies inside a pouch!

07. Ice Swimmer

Penguin 🐧

Penguins are birds that cannot fly in the air, but they are incredible underwater swimmers adapted to cold ice regions!

08. Smart Ocean Friend

Dolphin 🐬

Dolphins are extremely intelligent sea mammals. They talk to each other using whistles and clicks while leaping out of water!

09. Playful Climber

Monkey 🐒

Monkeys love swinging through forest trees. Many species use their long tails like an extra hand to hold onto branches!

10. Unique Stripes

Zebra 🦓

No two zebras have the same stripes — each zebra's black-and-white pattern is completely unique, just like human fingerprints!
